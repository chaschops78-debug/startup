# Entrepreneur Mentor

A persistent Claude skill that acts as a sharp, experienced entrepreneurial mentor — combining 8 time-tested frameworks with structured notes from 10 foundational entrepreneurship books. Designed to push back on thinking, not to flatter.

## What it does

- Applies decision-making frameworks (Thiel, Helmer, Munger, Bezos, Klein, Christensen, Fitzpatrick, Graham) to your specific situation.
- Draws on structured notes from 10 foundational entrepreneurship books.
- Supports "voice modes" (Munger mode, Thiel mode, Bezos mode, Buffett mode) for when you want a specific lens.
- Asks clarifying questions before advising — refuses to give generic advice.
- Ends every interaction with a concrete next action.

## How to install

1. Download `SKILL.md` and `books.md` from this folder.
2. In Claude → Settings → Skills, upload `SKILL.md`.
3. Trigger by saying:
   - "Mentor, I'm thinking about..."
   - "What should I do about [decision]?"
   - "Challenge me on this idea"
   - "Munger mode: should I do X?"
   - "Let me bounce something off you"

## Books in the mentor's brain

Structured notes for all 10 are in `books.md`. The mentor consults the right one(s) based on what you bring to him.

| Book | Author | What the mentor uses it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Principles | Ray Dalio | Decision systems, learning from failure, building team cultures |
| Crossing the Chasm | Geoffrey Moore | Go-to-market strategy, why early traction doesn't translate to scale |
| The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People | Stephen Covey | Personal effectiveness, prioritization, leadership style |
| Zero to One | Peter Thiel | Idea evaluation, monopoly thinking, distribution |
| The Lean Startup | Eric Ries | Iteration cycles, MVPs, pivot-vs-persevere decisions |
| Obviously Awesome | April Dunford | Positioning, messaging, why customers don't "get it" |
| The Hard Thing About Hard Things | Ben Horowitz | Crisis leadership, firing, hard people decisions |
| The Mom Test | Rob Fitzpatrick | Customer discovery, validating an idea without fooling yourself |
| Inside the Tornado | Geoffrey Moore | Scaling, hypergrowth strategy, post-PMF transitions |
| The Gorilla Game | Moore / Johnson / Kippola | Identifying winner-take-most platform markets |

## Growing the mentor over time

The mentor gets sharper as you add to his brain:

- **More books** — read one, give the rough takeaways, and we'll add a structured note to `books.md`.
- **Personal context** — add a `personal/about-me.md` with your skills, goals, constraints, and active ideas. The mentor will reference it when advising you specifically.
- **Decision journal** — log decisions and rationale in `personal/decision-journal.md`. The mentor can refer back when patterns emerge.

## Note on the book content

The notes in `books.md` are structured summaries and framework extractions written for use by this mentor — they are not reproductions of the books. To get the full value of any book, read it. The notes are the operational layer that lets the mentor apply the ideas in conversation; they are not a substitute for the source material.
